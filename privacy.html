Privacy Policy — Mochikomi Bonsai

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Mochikomi Bonsai is a personal journal for caring for bonsai trees. It works first on your phone: you can use it fully with no account and no internet, and in that case your data never leaves your device.

What data we handle

  • Without an account: your trees, photos, notes, reminders and settings stay only on your phone.
  • If you create an account (optional, from Settings): your email address and your password (the password is stored securely by our account provider, never in plain text), your display name if you enter one, your language and text size, your notification time, and your city, state/province, country and time zone if you fill them in.
  • Your synced content: your trees and their history (work and notes), your tree photos, species you create yourself, your reminders and, if you book a workshop, only your name for the booking (we never ask for your email or phone to book).
  • Weather: to show you the weather and frost or heat alerts, the app sends the city/location you configured to our weather provider. We do not send your name or your email with that query.
  • What we do NOT do: we use no trackers, show no ads, sell none of your data, and use no artificial intelligence with your data — reminders and suggestions are rules inside the app itself.

Who receives data (processors)

  • Supabase — stores the accounts, the database and the photos of those who create an account.
  • Open-Meteo — receives the configured location to return the weather.

Your controls, inside the app (Settings)

  • Export my data: you can take your data with you (a JSON file and a ZIP backup with your photos).
  • Delete all my data: deletes your data and photos from this phone and, if you are signed in, from the cloud as well.
  • Delete your account: you can request it from the public account deletion page by writing to us from your account email at bonsaisoulwisconsin@gmail.com; when the account is deleted, the associated data is deleted too.

Retention: your data is kept while you have your account or the app installed; when you delete your data or your account, it is removed as described above.

Children: the app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes: if this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this same page with its date.
